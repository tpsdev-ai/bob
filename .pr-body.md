Closes #222: systemd `ExecStart` arguments containing `%h` or `${HOME}` are no longer subject to specifier expansion or environment substitution — double-quoting prevents systemd interpreter substitution; additionally any `%` or `$` in ExecStart arguments is doubled so systemd passes them literally.

**Changed**

- `src/shell/service.ts`: the systemd renderer double-quotes every `ExecStart=` argument; backslash-escapes `\` and `"`, and doubles `%` and `$` so systemd passes them through literally (systemd.service: `%%` is literal `%`, `$$` is literal `$`).
- `test/shell/service.test.ts`: new test verifies a bob path with `%h` and `${HOME}` renders an ExecStart whose decoded argument (undoing quoting and `%%`/`$$` escaping) equals the original path.
