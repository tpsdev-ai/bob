- **`totalTimeoutMs` bounds the whole provider call, retries included (bob#313).** The total cap was enforced per attempt, so with retries configured a stalled call could run to a multiple of it: each attempt armed the cap from zero, and a retry's backoff sleep was not bounded at all. The cap is now one deadline set when the call starts — an attempt's total timer is clamped to the time left, a deadline already past aborts at once, and aborting the caller's signal also ends a retry's backoff sleep — so the call ends at or just after the deadline with the usual `ProviderRequestTimeoutError`. Rows with zero retries are unchanged. (`src/shell/base-url-transport.ts`, `src/shell/provider-request-policy.ts`, `test/shell/provider-total-timeout-313.test.ts`)
