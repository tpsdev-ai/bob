- **Provider-aware request timeouts and retry policy (bob#185 item 1).** Keyless rows may declare `request: {idleTimeoutMs, totalTimeoutMs, maxRetries}`; invalid policies refuse by row name. Idle timeouts limit waits for response headers or a body chunk, excluding consumer pauses; total timeouts cap the request. Row timeouts retain the provider, limit and remedy and are terminal; provider request retries obey `maxRetries`, and pi session retries are disabled for policy-bearing rows. Rows without a policy retain their timeout and retry settings. Refs #185.
