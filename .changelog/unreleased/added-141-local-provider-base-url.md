- **`provider.base_url` points an agent at a keyless local model endpoint, and is refused for any provider that carries a key.** `bob onboard`/`bob init` takes `--base-url <url>` for `ollama` on a non-`ollama.com` host, `ollama-newton` or `omlx`, writes it to `bob.yaml` and `.pi-agent/models.json`, and declares the model there; a keyed provider with a `base_url` is refused at load naming the rule, and the URL must be absolute http/https with no credentials.
