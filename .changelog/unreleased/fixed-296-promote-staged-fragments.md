- **`promote` stages the fragments it folds, so a fragment rewritten at its path mid-run is kept, not lost (bob#296).** It renames fragments into `.changelog/promote-staging`, folds the section from the moved bytes once they hash to the index blob, and deletes them only after the changelog write succeeds. A fragment changed before the move is refused; staged fragments are restored or left in the staging directory and named. A fragment recreated at its path after the move is kept and reported.
