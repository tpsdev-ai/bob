- **`promote` stages the fragments it folds, so a fragment rewritten at its path mid-run is kept, not lost (bob#296).** It moves every fragment into a private staging directory with a rename, folds the section from the moved bytes once they hash to the index blob, and deletes them only after the changelog write succeeds. A fragment changed before the move is moved back and refused; one rewritten at its path after it reappears at its path and `promote` reports it as kept.
