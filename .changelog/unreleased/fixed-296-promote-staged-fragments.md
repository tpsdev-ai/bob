- **`promote` stages the fragments it folds, so a fragment rewritten at its path mid-run is kept, not lost (bob#296).** It renames fragments into `.changelog/promote-staging`, folds the section from the moved bytes once they hash to the index blob, and deletes them only after the changelog write succeeds. A fragment changed before the move is refused. Rollback links staged fragments to their original paths, then unlinks the staged names: a link failure (including when the original path reappears) leaves the copy in staging and names it; an unlink failure after linking succeeds leaves it at both paths and names it. A fragment recreated at its path after the move is kept and reported on a successful promote.
