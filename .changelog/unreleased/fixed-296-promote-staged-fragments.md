- **`promote` stages the fragments it folds (bob#296).** It renames fragments into `.changelog/promote-staging`, folds the section from the moved bytes once they hash to the index blob, and deletes them only after the changelog write succeeds. A fragment changed before the move is refused. On any failure, promote stops before deleting what it could not account for, names the paths it could not restore, and leaves `.changelog/promote-staging` for inspection; the next promote refuses until that directory is removed. A fragment recreated at its path after the move is kept and reported on a successful promote.
