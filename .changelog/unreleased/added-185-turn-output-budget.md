- **Per-turn reasoning / output budget from the selected provider row (bob#185 item 2).** Keyless rows may declare `budget: {maxOutputTokens, reasoning}`; budgets on other rows and invalid budgets refuse by row name. bob hands the cap to pi as `maxTokens`, sent as `max_completion_tokens` (or `max_tokens` where the model's compat selects it), and the level as pi's thinking level, sent as `reasoning_effort` (`off` sends none). The built-in keyless rows use a 4096-token cap and the `low` level; rows without a budget are unchanged. A request that ends with stop reason `length` carries `outputCap` in the run log's request-usage record. Refs #185.
