- **A finished mail launcher no longer waits for the turn timeout when an unkillable group member holds its output open (#208).** After process-group reaping gives up, result collection waits for a short bounded interval and then applies the normal completeness rule to the bytes already read: a complete result stands without retry, while an incomplete result remains a failed turn. The exceptional collection is logged with the group id, counted as `resultCollectedAfterReapExhausted`, and shown by `bob doctor` beside `reapExhausted`.
